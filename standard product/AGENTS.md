rules. Customer-specific rules should not be promoted here unless the user
confirms that the behavior is product-wide.

2. Read `D:\Workspace\standard product\01-requirements\00-project-overview\project-context.md`.
3. Read `D:\Workspace\standard product\01-requirements\01-source-documents\source-index.md`.
