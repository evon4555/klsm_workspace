# Company Source Document Index

## Purpose

This folder stores original company-level source documents.

These files provide general business and terminology background. They are not
project requirements and should not override project-specific or
requirement-specific source documents.

## Source Files

### Onboarding

- `onboarding\2025Q3新员工培训20250818.pptx`

Usage:

- company business background
- representative customers and scenarios
- domain and product positioning

### Terminology

- `terminology\AnTank系统的行业术语库.xlsx`

Usage:

- common business terminology
- Chinese and English naming references
- terminology alignment for requirements, test cases, and reports

## Update Rule

- Keep original source files unchanged.
- Put stable extracted context under `..\00-company-overview`.
- Put project-specific or requirement-specific facts under the relevant project
  or requirement folder instead.
