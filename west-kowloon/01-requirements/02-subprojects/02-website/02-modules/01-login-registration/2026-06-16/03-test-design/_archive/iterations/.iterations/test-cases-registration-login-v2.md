## [SECTION] Scope Summary

- Feature: Website registration and login.
- Source authority: **Mindmap > PRD** for this project. Mindmap registration/login sub-tree at `..\..\01-input\02-mindmap\2026-05-26\{注册,登录1,登录2,忘记密码}.png` is authoritative. PRD `..\..\..\..\..\..\01-source-documents\02-prd\2026-05-14\国际版标准官网 - 在线购票.pdf` §3 is supplementary reference (Website-level, not requirement-narrow). PRD-only items (auto-OTP-registration, 5-min guest popup, SSO three-jump) are also in scope per user direction (PRD-fills-gap rule).
- Requirement: REQ-WEB-REG-001 to REQ-WEB-REG-006; plus mindmap-confirmed flows (forgot password, captcha on all flows, OTP-mode login, email-link registration) and PRD-confirmed flows (SSO three-jump, auto-OTP-registration, 5-min guest popup).
- Risk level: High.
- Assumptions: 5 third-party providers confirmed: **Google, Facebook, X, Tiktok, wechat** (no Apple). Mobile registration/login are mindmap placeholders (yellow) — cases authored and marked **Deferred — HK-side verification** until detail is provided.
- Open issues: see linked risk review for full Open Questions; the most blocking for execution are session-expiry values, OTP-mode credentials, captcha exact behavior, lockout thresholds, and SSO dry-run scope decision.
