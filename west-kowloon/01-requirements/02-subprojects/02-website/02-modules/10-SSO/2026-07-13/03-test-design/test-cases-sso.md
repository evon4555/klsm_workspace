## [SECTION] Scope Summary

- Feature: Unified C-end SSO with AnTank as the single customer-facing login entry and OAuth IdP.
- Current design set: v1, 24 SIT cases, aligned with the signed consolidation document.
- Signed scope: `../02-analysis/requirement-consolidation-sso.md`.
- Execution target for this round: local mock SP `http://127.0.0.1:6080/login`.
- Related old cases: `SIT-TC-WEB-AUTH-014` and `SIT-TC-WEB-AUTH-039..041` are regression references only and are not modified by this package.
- Out of scope for this round: real external SP sites, full WestK Customer Portal API contract testing, CRM deep sync, AuthCode expiry SLA, QPS/performance, and App WebView.
- Markdown mirrors the project TestCase_Template.xlsx columns; execution-result fields remain blank at design time.

- SP mock entry/configuration: SSO-001..004
